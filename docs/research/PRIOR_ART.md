## Stop rule check

The specific gaps targeted by the work packages are (a) an open, receiver independent, advisory trust layer scored
against PX4's own estimator decisions on real flights, (b) detection of a velocity consistent drift without an
independent reference, and (c) latency on slow drift. No source above closes (a): PX4 offers a receiver flag and
innovation gates, ArduPilot a glitch radius, and neither is scored against drift. Rows 8 and 9 show that detection from
onboard sensors is an established idea, so **Marsad does not claim novelty for the idea of consistency checks**; the
claim is narrowed to the evidence base, the training free design and the honest comparison with the estimator that
flies the vehicle. For (b) and (c) the prior art offers no free tool that applies to this problem without a
reference sensor, so the work packages stay as planned.
